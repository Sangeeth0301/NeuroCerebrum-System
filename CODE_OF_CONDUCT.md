# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticise ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks or publishing others' private information.
- Respect patient privacy: never share identifiable clinical data, even if you have access to it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers through the repository's issue tracker (marked private where possible) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.
