# Sun et al. 2022 (PNAS) — DeepSIF: deep neural networks constrained by neural mass models for EEG source imaging

## Citation & Link
- Rui Sun, Abbas Sohrabpour, Gregory A. Worrell, Bin He. "Deep neural networks constrained by neural mass models improve electrophysiological source imaging of spatiotemporal brain dynamics." *PNAS* 119(31), e2201128119, 2022. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2201128119 — https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2201128119
- Open access (PMC9351497, CC BY-NC-ND): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9351497/ ; metadata https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/10.1073/pnas.2201128119
- Note: the title does not literally say "DeepSIF"; DeepSIF = Deep-learning-based Source Imaging Framework as named in the paper.

## Venue type
Peer-reviewed journal (PNAS). Numbers read via the PMC full text.

## Problem
Electrophysiological source imaging (ESI) from scalp EEG is ill-posed and conventional solvers (sLORETA, beamformers, etc.) require expert hyperparameter tuning. Goal: a trained network that maps scalp EEG to cortical source activity (location, extent, time course) with minimal user intervention. — https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/10.1073/pnas.2201128119

## Dataset
- **Training**: purely synthetic — cortical source space split into 994 regions (template head); source patches of varying location/size/shape generated by growing a random seed region; activity simulated with neural mass models; projected to sensors via a lead field. — https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2201128119 (as summarized in search results)
- **Patients**: 20 drug-resistant focal epilepsy patients (14 F; 34±14 y), resective surgery with ≥1 year seizure freedom; **76-channel high-density EEG, interictal spikes** (18±20 spikes/patient). — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9351497/
- **Healthy**: 20 subjects in 3 public datasets (VEP, SEP, visual oddball P300). — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9351497/

## Approach
Spatial module + temporal (recurrent) module network trained on simulated source/sensor pairs with added noise; at inference, scalp EEG → 994-region source time courses; thresholded to obtain an imaged region. Compared with sLORETA, LCMV beamformer, cMEM, FAST-IRES. — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9351497/

## Evaluation protocol
- Simulation: localization error (LE), temporal correlation, extent precision/recall.
- Patients: imaged region vs. **surgical resection** (precision, recall, spatial dispersion) and, in 6 patients with CT-localized intracranial electrodes, distance to iEEG **SOZ**.
- No train/test leakage issue in the ML sense (trained on simulations only, tested on real patients), but the head model is a template, not patient-specific.

## Key results (numbers)
From https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9351497/
- Simulation: LE **1.41±1.87 mm** overall (~6 mm for deep sources); temporal correlation 0.99±0.02 (1 source), 0.91±0.16 (2), 0.85±0.19 (3); extent precision 0.81±0.17, recall 0.85±0.18.
- Patients (interictal spikes vs resection): precision **0.79±0.31** (median 0.93), recall **0.49±0.25**, spatial dispersion **3.87±7.92 mm**; SOZ localization error (n=6) **7.45±8.91 mm**.
- Significantly better localization/extent than sLORETA, beamformer, cMEM; comparable to FAST-IRES but thousands of times faster (tens of ms vs minutes).
- Correct localization of VEP P100 (BA17/18) and SEP P30 (BA1-3) in healthy subjects.
- Caution: some secondary sources/search snippets attribute the numbers "specificity 96%, dispersion 3.80±5.74 mm, 10.89±10.14 mm to SOZ" to this PNAS paper; those numbers are actually from the 2024 *Advanced Science* ictal follow-up (see C_2024_Sun_DeepSIF_ictal_AdvSci.md) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11653641/

## Limitations
- Interictal spikes only (not seizures); cohort mostly temporal lobe epilepsy; resection is a coarse ground truth (larger than epileptogenic zone); template head model; needs retraining for large lesions/prior resections. — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9351497/
- Requires high-density (76-ch) EEG; clinical 19-ch TUSZ recordings are much sparser (performance under 10-20 montages is examined in later work, e.g. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12629399/ — not reviewed here).

## Relevance to our project
- Offers a route from scalp channels to **cortical regions** (994 regions → lobes), which is what a "brain-region spread map" really needs; a trained DeepSIF-like model could be applied per time window to animate spread.
- For a student project on TUSZ (19 channels, no MRI), full ESI is likely out of scope; a simpler channel → lobe mapping is more realistic, with DeepSIF cited as the principled upper-bound approach.
