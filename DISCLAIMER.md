# Disclaimer

NeuroMech-Warn is a **research prototype**.

- It is **not a medical device** and has not been cleared or approved by any regulator (FDA, CE, CDSCO or others).
- It must **not** be used to diagnose, treat, monitor or make decisions about any patient.
- Seizure warnings, seizure types, onset zones, spread maps and body-reaction estimates produced by this software are **experimental model outputs**. They can be wrong, late or missing.
- Body reactions other than heart rate and muscle activity are **inferred** from seizure type and spread, not measured.
- "Virtual neurons" and excitation/inhibition estimates are **model-based simulations** of large neural populations driven by scalp EEG. They are not recordings of real neurons.
- Results are reported on public research datasets (TUSZ, CHB-MIT, Siena) and may not generalise to other hospitals, devices or patients.

Anyone with epilepsy should follow the advice of their own clinician.

The datasets used by this project are provided by their owners under their own terms. This repository does not redistribute them.
